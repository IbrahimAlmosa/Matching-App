## Prototype D · Together (after client feedback, 2026-10-23)
Nichole liked A and C and asked for one version that works on both phones and desktops, plus a sign-up page where volunteers choose their preferences. D combines the two:
- Live link: https://claude.ai/artifact/HzgTnUkjyp2jaNeMCxJJp4 · File: voluntr-together.html
- From A: the one-card-at-a-time opportunity flow, the match ring and score breakdown, tab navigation, and the navy and marigold colors.
- From C: Atkinson Hyperlegible type, a text-size control, task-based home screens for each role, plain-language "why we suggested this" reasons, and the PCC phone line.
- New: the layout adapts to the screen (bottom tabs on phones, a left menu with wider two-column cards on desktop), with a Phone/Desktop switch for demos. There is also a public sign-up page that leads into a 6-step sign-up with a "your preferences so far" panel on desktop and a review step at the end.

## Use cases covered (all three)
UC-VOL-01 to 05, UC-ORG-01 to 05, UC-MAT-01 to 03 (score breakdown, hidden ineligible matches, weights), UC-CON-01, 02 and 04 (contact details hidden until mutual acceptance), UC-ADM-01 to 05, UC-NFR-01, 02, 03, 05, 07 and 08. Admin MFA is shown in all three. Hour tracking is left out because the CRD lists it as future phase. It conflicts with the Sprint 2 answers, so confirm it with Nichole.
